Project Overview

Bridging the gap between producers and buyers.

The problem

Many farmers in Uganda struggle to access up-to-date market information, while buyers often lack dependable ways to discover available produce and compare prices.

Our solution

Smart Market provides a simple mobile platform for listing crops, tracking commodity prices, reviewing market trends, and reaching out directly to buyers or sellers.

Key Features

Everything needed to support a better market experience.

Price Tracking

Users can view commodity prices across 11 markets in Uganda and compare current rates quickly.

Trend Charts

The app displays price trend charts that make it easier to spot changes over time and make informed decisions.

Farmer Listings

Farmers can publish listings with images, descriptions, and contact details to reach interested buyers.

Direct Communication

Buyers and farmers can connect directly through the app to discuss products and transactions.

Why It Matters

Supporting transparency and access in local markets.

Project impact

Smart Market aims to make agricultural trade more transparent and efficient by giving users access to practical data and direct communication tools in one place.

  • Improves visibility of market prices
  • Encourages faster connection between stakeholders
  • Supports informed buying and selling decisions

Built with

Flutter for the mobile experience, Firebase for data storage and authentication, and a clean user interface focused on usability.
